FAQ

Common Questions

Everything you need to know before booking a call.

What exactly does Aesthera do?
We build your medspa a fully branded mobile app that your patients download to their phones. Inside, they can browse and purchase treatments, manage memberships, earn loyalty rewards, and receive personalized offers — all under your brand.
Does this replace my EMR or booking system?
No. Aesthera works alongside your existing tools — Aesthetic Record, Vagaro, Boulevard, or whatever you use. We're a patient-facing loyalty and mobile commerce layer, not a practice management replacement.
How long does setup take?
Most apps are live within 48 hours. We handle all configuration — your branding, treatments, pricing, payment processing, and rewards program. You just review and approve.
Is this a good fit for a solo medspa?
It's designed specifically for solo, owner-operated medspas. You don't need a marketing team or technical staff. The app runs automated campaigns and rewards while you focus on treating patients.
What kind of support do I get?
Aesthera offers hands-on, dedicated support from day one — no ticket systems, no chatbots, no waiting. We handle your entire setup, walk you through onboarding personally, and stay available as your practice grows. You're not a customer. You're a partner.
